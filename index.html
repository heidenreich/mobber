MOBBER

◆ ◆ ◆

The city sleeps. By dawn, it'll be yours.

  • Move your Don with WASD / arrow keys, or hold the mouse or a finger where you want to go.
  • Walk near street toughs to bring them into the family. A bigger crew means more pull.
  • Park your Don inside a block with more muscle than anyone else to take the turf. Every block you own pays protection money.
  • Rival families want the same blocks. When crews collide, the bigger crew wins the brawl.
  • Muscle draws heat, and heat brings cops. Space: bribe nearby cops ($150). E: hire 4 goons ($200).
  • Hold enough blocks before dawn, and before a rival family does.
