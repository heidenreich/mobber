MOBBER

Start a mob. Spread the word. Launch the message.

  • Move with WASD / arrow keys, or hold the mouse or a finger where you want to go.
  • Stand near bystanders to recruit them. A bigger mob gives you a bigger hype radius.
  • Rival mobs steal your followers if theirs is bigger. If yours is bigger, you can steal theirs.
  • Buzzkills (red) talk your followers out of it. Press Space / RALLY to blast your megaphone and scare them off.
  • Reach the mob size before the clock runs out and your message goes out.
